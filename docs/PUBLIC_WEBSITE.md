# Public School Website Documentation (`apps/website`)

## Purpose
The Public School Website is designed exclusively for prospective students, parents, alumni, and general visitors to explore school offerings, view notices, submit admission enquiries, and contact school administration.

## Key Pages & Navigation
- **Home (`/`)**: Hero banner, quick notice ticker, principal message preview, campus stats, upcoming events.
- **About Us (`/about`)**: School history (est. 1998), vision, mission, and core values.
- **Academics (`/academics`)**: Primary, middle, and senior high curriculum details.
- **Faculty (`/faculty`)**: Public teacher profiles (Photo, Name, Designation, Department, Qualification, Subject, Experience). *No login or dashboard provided.*
- **Facilities (`/facilities`)**: Science labs, STEM robotics lab, smart library, sports complex, cafeteria, transport.
- **Admissions (`/admissions`)**: Admission procedure, fee structure overview, interactive Online Admission Enquiry Form.
- **Events & Notices (`/events`)**: School events calendar, filterable news feed, circular announcements.
- **Gallery (`/gallery`)**: Photo & video albums with interactive lightbox preview.
- **Achievements (`/achievements`)**: Student awards, teacher recognitions, board exam performance.
- **Downloads (`/downloads`)**: Circulars, syllabus PDFs, admission prospectus forms.
- **Calendar (`/calendar`)**: Term dates, exam schedules, official school holidays.
- **FAQ (`/faq`)**: Accordion answers to common admission and campus questions.
- **Contact (`/contact`)**: Campus address, office phone, email, Google Maps embed, and inquiry form.
- **Policies (`/policies`)**: Child safety policy, data privacy, code of conduct.
- **AI School Assistant**: Floating chatbot modal answering visitor queries.
- **Admin Login Link**: Discrete secure link in footer pointing to Admin Portal.
